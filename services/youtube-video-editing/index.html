A simple editing process

01 — Brief

Send the footage or a link to the files, target length, references and deadline. I reply within 1–2 business days with questions and a clear plan.

02 — Edit & review

I build the cut, then share a review link. We agree revision rounds up front so feedback stays focused and the project keeps moving.

03 — Finish & deliver

I complete the sound, colour and captions, then deliver the final exports for YouTube and any cutdowns you have commissioned.
