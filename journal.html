A curious eye.
A craft in the making.

I am Aderemi Abdullah Salako, the person behind Remi Visuals. Based in Abuja, Nigeria, I am a self-taught creative professional building a practice in editing, photography, videography, and visual storytelling.

I’m drawn to what happens between frames: the pause that makes a scene breathe, the cut that gives a story momentum, and the colour that changes how a moment feels. My learning happens through making, revisiting, and refining.

DaVinci Resolve is the centre of my editing desk. I work with Fusion compositing, colour grading, subtitles, sound cleanup, and social-media-first storytelling. AI-assisted media workflows support exploration and production, with human judgement guiding the final edit.

My ambition is to grow through thoughtful freelance collaborations and build Remi Visuals into a studio with a clear point of view: purposeful images, careful listening, and stories people remember.

❧ Creative Process

A clear path from rough brief to finished story.

  1. 01

    Brief

    Define goals, audience, references, timeline, and the intended delivery channels.

  2. 02

    Story & Structure

    Shape the strongest sequence, rhythm, and narrative logic from the available material.

  3. 03

    Edit, VFX & Sound

    Refine the cut, pacing, captions, composites, sound bed, transitions, and motion accents.

  4. 04

    Colour, 3D & Polish

    Balance footage, create the look, add 3D or VFX elements where needed, and prepare final exports.

  5. 05

    Delivery

    Package files for social, web, broadcast, archive, or client handoff.
