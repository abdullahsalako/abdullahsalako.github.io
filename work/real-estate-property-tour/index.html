Real Estate Property Tour

A vertical property tour for TikTok and Instagram Reels: a sharp hook, word-timed captions, a quick room-by-room walkthrough and one clear prompt to book a private showing.

The brief

Produce a sleek vertical property highlight reel from 48 seconds of raw walkthrough footage, built to earn attention in the first three seconds and drive qualified buyer inquiries.

The approach

The opening states the facts the presenter says out loud: four bedrooms, three bathrooms, one story. Her intro stays in sync with word-timed captions. The silent middle of the walk-through becomes seven short shots with slow push-ins and room labels, and the closing line lands on a single booking prompt.
