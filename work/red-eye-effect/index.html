Red Eye Effect

A cinematic colour study that turns a quiet close-up into an unsettling red-eye reveal through restrained grading and selective colour work.

The brief

Build tension without turning the frame into an effect. The image needed to feel intimate first, then quietly strange — a look that rewards a second viewing.

The approach

The treatment keeps the surrounding palette restrained and the contrast controlled, reserving saturation for the eye reveal. Each adjustment was chosen to protect the skin tone and let the emotional shift arrive through colour rather than noise.
