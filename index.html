❧ Page Three · Magazine Profile

The Story Behind the Cut.

I am Aderemi Abdullah Salako, also known professionally through Remi Visuals, based in Abuja, Nigeria. I work at the intersection of video editing, VFX, 3D animation, motion graphics, and creative technology, creating thoughtful visual work that feels cinematic while keeping the message clear.

My post-production process uses a DaVinci Resolve workflow for assembly, sound, colour, compositing, and finishing, supported by 3D animation and AI-assisted production where the story needs it. Whether the job is a short-form campaign, a visual identity piece, a VFX shot, or an event story, I focus on structure, texture, and emotional clarity.

❧ Creative Process

A clear path from rough brief to finished story.

  1. 01

    Brief

    Define goals, audience, references, timeline, and the intended delivery channels.

  2. 02

    Story & Structure

    Shape the strongest sequence, rhythm, and narrative logic from the available material.

  3. 03

    Edit, VFX & Sound

    Refine the cut, pacing, captions, composites, sound bed, transitions, and motion accents.

  4. 04

    Colour, 3D & Polish

    Balance footage, create the look, add 3D or VFX elements where needed, and prepare final exports.

  5. 05

    Delivery

    Package files for social, web, broadcast, archive, or client handoff.
