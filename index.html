The Story Behind the Cut.

I am Aderemi Abdullah Salako, also known professionally through Remi Visuals, based in Abuja, Nigeria. I work at the intersection of editing, motion, photography, and creative technology, creating thoughtful visual work that feels cinematic while keeping the message clear.

My post-production process uses a DaVinci Resolve workflow for assembly, sound, colour, and finishing. Whether the job is a short-form campaign, a visual identity piece, or an event story, I focus on structure, texture, and emotional clarity.

My Creative Process

A clear path from rough brief to finished story.
