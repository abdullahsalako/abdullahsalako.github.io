About Aderemi A. Salako, video editor and colourist in Nigeria

A practice built through making, revisiting, and refining.

I am Aderemi A. Salako, the person behind Remi Visuals. I’m a self-taught video editor, colourist and content strategist based in Nigeria, available for freelance, remote and contract projects worldwide. My practice spans editing, colour, photography, videography and visual storytelling for creators, brands and agencies.

DaVinci Resolve is the centre of my editing desk — Fusion compositing, colour grading, subtitles, sound cleanup, and social-first storytelling, with AI-assisted workflows supporting exploration while human judgement guides the final edit.
