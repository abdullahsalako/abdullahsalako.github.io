Services

Video editing, colour grading and content strategy

I edit and grade in DaVinci Resolve for YouTube creators, brands and agencies. I take on freelance, remote and contract work for clients worldwide.

YouTube video editing

Long-form edits that keep viewers watching: a clear structure, a strong opening, pacing that changes when attention would drop, and a finish that sets up the next video.

  • Story structure, cutdown and pacing from raw footage
  • Captions, lower thirds and on-screen text
  • Dialogue cleanup, music and sound effects
  • Colour correction so every episode looks consistent

Short-form & social media content

Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts built for the feed: a hook in the first three seconds, fast but readable cuts, and captions designed as part of the frame so the video works with the sound off.

  • Vertical edits and cutdowns from long-form videos
  • Caption and text design for silent viewing
  • Series formats that can be repeated every week

Colour grading in DaVinci Resolve

Colour correction and creative grading that make footage feel intentional without looking filtered. I build grades as structured node trees, so they’re repeatable across a series and skin tones stay natural.

  • Exposure and white-balance correction, and shot matching
  • LOG footage transforms and colour-managed delivery
  • Cinematic looks, skin-tone isolation and film grain

Content strategy

Editing works best when the plan behind it is clear. I help creators and brands shape formats, hooks and a publishing rhythm, so one shoot becomes a series rather than a one-off post.

  • Format and series planning for YouTube and social
  • Hook, structure and retention reviews of existing videos
  • Repurposing plans that turn long-form into short-form

Motion graphics & Fusion compositing

Titles, animated text, graphic accents and practical VFX built in DaVinci Resolve Fusion, designed to support the edit instead of competing with it.

Subtitles & audio cleanup

Accurate subtitles styled to match the video, plus dialogue cleanup and level balancing so the final export is ready to publish on every platform.

How I work

Freelance, remote or on contract, worldwide.

1. Brief

You send the footage or idea, the platform, length and deadline. I reply within 1–2 business days with questions and a plan.

2. Edit & grade

I cut, grade and finish in DaVinci Resolve and share a review link, with a set number of revision rounds agreed up front.

3. Deliver

You get platform-ready exports, and project files on request, in the formats and aspect ratios you need.
